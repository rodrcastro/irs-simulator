IRS brackets —

Rates apply to taxable income (rendimento coletável). That's gross income minus the specific deduction, which is the larger of €4,587.09 or your Social Security contributions. Azores and Madeira apply a 30% reduction on mainland rates.

Key parameters

Assumptions & limits

  • November 2026 relief: the cut of 0.3–0.5 p.p. on brackets 1–6 is retroactive to 1 Jan 2026. In November only, withholding on salary and the Christmas subsidy is cut by 50%. December goes back to the regular 2026 tables. The rest of the gain arrives with the 2027 IRS settlement.
  • 2027: uses the OE2027 proposal, with bracket limits +3.88% and the reduced rates kept. The final vote is expected on 24 Nov 2026, so values may still change. The specific deduction (8.54 × IAS) and IRS Jovem cap (55 × IAS) depend on the 2027 IAS, which is an editable assumption. Minimum existence is €13,580.
  • Regions: monthly withholding uses the official 2026 regional tables (Despacho 1179/2026 for the Azores, Despacho 19/2026 for Madeira). Annual tax assumes the 30% regional reduction applies to the revised and 2027 rates as well.
  • IRS Jovem: the exempt income still counts when setting the tax rate for the rest of your income. In the payslip, the exempt share of each payment is excluded from withholding.
  • Dependents: €600 each. €726 if ≤ 6 years, rising to €900 from the second child ≤ 6. With 3+ dependents, withholding drops 1 p.p.
  • Married: withholding uses Table III if the spouse has no income, otherwise Table I. With separate returns, dependent and expense deductions are split 50/50. Joint tax is attributed to each spouse in proportion to their taxable income.
  • Employees (Category A) only. Not modelled: disability tables, overtime, meal allowance, other income categories, NHR/IFICI, and the full art. 70 minimum-existence formula, which is approximated as "net income can't fall below the minimum".
  • This is an estimate. Your employer's payroll software and the official AT settlement may differ by a few euros.
